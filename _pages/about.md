About Me
======

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the [Siebel School of Computing and Data Science](https://siebelschool.illinois.edu/) at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/) working under [Prof. Saugata Ghose](https://ghose.cs.illinois.edu/index.html).
My research interests are in the broad area of computer architecture, with particular emphasis in memory and storage systems, as well as accelerators for machine learning, scientific computing, and database systems. 

My work has been recognized with the University of Rochester Hopeman Fellowship and the University of Illinois Mavis Future Faculty Fellowship, and I have been named an MLCommons ML and Systems Rising Star and a Heidelberg Laureate Forum Young Researcher.
I have also received an Outstanding Teaching Assistant Award from the University of Illinois.

Outside of research, I am actively involved in the [Computer Architecture Student Association (CASA)](https://www.sigarch.org/casa/), where I serve as co-chair (2026–27) and have been a member of the Steering Committee since 2022.

Prior to coming to Illinois, I received a B.S. in Computer Science, a B.A. in Chemistry, and an M.S. in Electrical Engineering all from the [University of Rochester](https://rochester.edu/).
At the U of R, I studied under [Prof. Engin Ipek](https://www.cs.rochester.edu/u/ipek/Home.html), where I worked on a variety of research projects ranging from energy-efficient memory systems to processing-in/using-memory.
